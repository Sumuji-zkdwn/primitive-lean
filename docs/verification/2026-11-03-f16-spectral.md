# F16 spectral projector bridge (2026-11-03)

A rational, exact spectral description of the signed Cayley-Dickson left pencil
L_t = L_3 + t L_10. No association of the nonassociative algebra with
a field extension or automatic geometric holonomy is claimed.

Let K = L_3^T L_10 + L_10^T L_3. An exact finite calculation of the
original sign table shows K has four signed, symmetric off-diagonal pairs,
with 8 other coordinates absent. Let D be the diagonal projector onto
the eight coupled coordinates. Then the spectral projectors are

P_+ = D/2 + K/4, P_- = D/2 - K/4, P_0 = I-D.

The checked exact identities assert that these three matrices sum to I,
are mutually orthogonal, idempotent and symmetric, have ranks 4,4,8,
and K acts with respective eigenvalues 2,-2,0.

Hence H_t = L_t^T L_t has eigenvalues
(1+t)^2 (4), (1-t)^2 (4), (1+t^2) (8).

This supplement checks rational matrix identities and carries the original
sign table to an explicit projector system; a succeeding real-valued source
will state the parameter-dependent Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse and prove
its identities. The independent Python checker compares H_t with its
rational spectral reconstruction at selected integer t and does not
substitute for the Lean verification.
