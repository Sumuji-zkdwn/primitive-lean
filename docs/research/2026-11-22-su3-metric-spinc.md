# Geometry-only gauge algebra, Dirac obstruction, and a Spin-c test

Date: 2026-11-22 JST. Status: research-stage exact algebra + index-arithmetic checks, not a Standard Model solution.

## Goal and input restriction

Attempt to reconstruct familiar field content from one metric/geometric structure, rather than postulating independent electromagnetic, weak or strong fields. Geometry is allowed to have additional compact dimensions, intrinsic topology and a Levi-Civita spin connection. **Do not silently add an independent U(1) gauge field, non-Abelian Yang–Mills action, fermion field, or prescribed matter representation and call this "pure gravity".** Ordinary spinors, spin-c structures, quantization and coupling choices require explicit additional justification. Mathematical realizability of one gauge connection is not a physical reconstruction.

## Exact 8-dimensional SU(3) Lie-group metric

Let K=SU(3). At its identity take the standard anti-Hermitian basis T_a=-iλ_a/2 from the Gell-Mann matrices (a=1,...,8), orthonormal for -2Tr(T_a T_b). Define a left-invariant Riemannian metric from the positive inner product with diagonal weights

    q=(1,1,1,2,2,2,2,1).

This weight distinguishes the 4 directions complementary to the u(2) subalgebra generated by T1,T2,T3,T8, while preserving that subalgebra. By construction, the entire left SU(3) group acts by isometries. Right translations preserve the left-invariant metric iff their adjoint action preserves Q; infinitesimally this is exactly

    ad_X^T Q + Q ad_X = 0.

Independent SymPy computations on **all eight explicit 3×3 complex matrices** verify:
- The trace normalization, Lie brackets and all 8×8 adjoint matrices exactly.
- The 64×8 system of infinitesimal isometry constraints has rank 4, nullspace exactly span(T1,T2,T3,T8).
- This right stabilizer closes under the bracket and has Lie algebra su(2)⊕u(1).
- Combined with the automatic left su(3), there is a **12-dimensional isometry Lie algebra** su(3)⊕su(2)⊕u(1), at least as a connected subgroup. A global quotient by finite central subgroups needs an additional group-level argument. This exact construction is an instance of a known approach, not a novel gauge unification theorem.

For this left-invariant metric, the compact-unimodular Ricci scalar identity yields

    scalar = -1/4 Σ_{abc} f_{ab}^c² q_c/(q_a q_b)
             -1/2 Σ_a Killing(T_a,T_a)/q_a.

Here the bracket sum is 39/2, Killing(T_a,T_a)=-3, and scalar curvature is exactly **33/8>0** (in the chosen metric normalization).

## Consequence: an honest fermion obstruction

SU(3) as a Lie group is parallelizable, hence spin. Positive scalar curvature implies, by the Schrödinger–Lichnerowicz formula, that the **ordinary, untwisted intrinsic Dirac operator** on this compact geometry has no zero modes.

This is metric-specific, but there is a stronger conventional obstruction: the Atiyah–Hirzebruch vanishing theorem states that on a compact spin manifold with a nontrivial smooth circle action, its untwisted Dirac index (Â genus) is zero. In fact on any compact parallelizable positive-dimensional manifold the positive-degree Pontryagin classes vanish, implying Â genus zero. This rules out *net untwisted Dirac chirality* on a naive metric-only compactification. It does **not** prove that all chiral constructions are impossible; different operator sectors, spin-c twists, gauge representations, non-Killing symmetries and noncompact/singular spaces change the hypotheses and need separate derivations.

## A nonzero index from intrinsic complex geometry (NOT yet the Standard Model)

A distinct geometric candidate is CP2 with the Fubini–Study Kähler structure. CP2 is not spin, since its tangent Chern class is c1=3H and w2=c1 mod2=H ≠0, but its canonical **Spin-c** structure exists, with determinant line L=K_CP2^-1=O(3). Unlike a newly postulated independent Maxwell field, the canonical determinant connection is induced by the Kähler/complex geometry; however the complex structure and spin-c interpretation are extra geometric structure compared with a bare arbitrary Lorentzian metric.

Use the standard 4D spin-c index formula

    ind(D_L^+) = (c1(L)^2 - signature(CP2))/8.

For signature(CP2)=1 and c1(L)=3H, with ∫H²=1, the index is

    ind(D_canonical^+) = (3²-1)/8 = 1.

If the spin-c structure is twisted by O(k), the determinant is O(3+2k), yielding

    ind(D_k^+) = ((2k+3)²-1)/8 = (k+1)(k+2)/2.

The canonical complex spin-c Dirac operator is identified with the Dolbeault operator, and its one positive-chirality zero mode is compatible with χ(O_CP2)=1. This **bypasses the untwisted Spin Dirac index obstruction by changing the operator/bundle class**, not by contradicting Atiyah–Hirzebruch. The existing external index theorem is a stated input; our Lean file proves only the polynomial identities, and the independent SymPy oracle checks their arithmetic. We have not computed a physically correct electroweak representation or demonstrated a single 12D metric with both this index and the desired gauge algebra.

## The next decisive obstruction test

Can one choose **a single internal space K**, a consistent high-dimensional gravitational action, and a geometrically induced (not separately postulated) chiral operator such that:
1. The exact **global** gauge group and physical SU(3) triplet / SU(2) doublet representations are obtained;
2. The equivariant Dirac index is nonzero in the **correct representations** without importing external gauge fluxes;
3. The full Einstein-reduced dynamics is consistent, and gauge/matter anomalies cancel;
4. The predicted spectrum and couplings agree with experimental Standard Model data?

Until these are met, "gravity-only reconstruction of existing physics" remains an open program. Current positive results are gauge/algebra geometry; the geometric Spin-c index is a separate, precisely bounded escape route.
