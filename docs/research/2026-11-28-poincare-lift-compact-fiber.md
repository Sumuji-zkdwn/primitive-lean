# 2026-11-28 — Compact-fiber Poincare lift forces the warped-product ansatz

## Bounded question and verdict

Continue the pure-vacuum compactification branch #179/#260. Can dropping the block-diagonal hypothesis alone evade the old no-go, if exact external Poincare symmetry is retained but may act nontrivially on the compactification fibers? Under the explicit equivariant-lift hypotheses below, NO: block diagonality is a consequence rather than an ansatz. This is a hand argument with exact finite algebra checks, using standard geometry, not a claim of a new published theorem.

Assume a smooth surjective submersion pi:M^(4+n)->R^(1,3), connected compact boundaryless fibers with positive definite induced metric, a smooth Lorentzian total metric of index one, and a genuine smooth isometric action of the connected Poincare group (or its connected spin cover) covering the standard affine action on the base. No product coordinates or metric-block hypothesis is imposed. Require Ric_M=0 only after the symmetry reduction. In particular the lifted translations must be an actual R^4 subgroup action, not merely four transformations commuting modulo internal gauge.

## 1. Equivariant product trivialization

Let K=pi^-1(0), and let T_x denote the lifted translation by x. Define Phi(x,y)=T_x y. Its inverse sends p to (pi(p),T_{-pi(p)}p), by the group law. Hence it is a global diffeomorphism. Translation invariance makes all coefficients of Phi* g independent of x. The lift of the connected Lorentz group restricts to an isometric action rho on the Riemannian metric of K. The semidirect product law gives L Phi(x,y)=Phi(Lx,rho(L)y). There is no assumption that rho was trivial.

## 2. A compact Riemannian fiber cannot absorb boosts

Pull back the invariant nonnegative inner product on the infinitesimal Killing fields, given by the integral over K of their pointwise metric inner product, along the infinitesimal Lorentz action. This gives a positive semidefinite symmetric form Q on so(1,3) invariant under the adjoint action; the sign convention for fundamental vector fields does not affect invariance. Compactness makes all integrals finite. In the basis of three rotations J_i and three boosts B_i, for each cyclic i,j,k, [B_i,B_j]=-J_k and [B_i,J_k]=-B_j. Thus B_j +/- J_k are real nonzero adjoint eigenvectors of ad B_i with eigenvalues +/-1 (in opposite order for the two signs). Ad invariance implies 2 lambda Q(X,X)=0 for either eigenvector X. Positivity implies its image is the zero Killing field. All six generators are spanned by these cyclic pairs and therefore their images vanish. A connected Lie group with zero infinitesimal action acts trivially. This also applies to the connected spin cover, including its center, not just SO^+(1,3).

Independent check: the symmetric 6x6 adjoint invariant Gram equations have rank 19 in 21 unknowns, and both basis matrices of the nullspace have zero ordinary trace. Every invariant Gram matrix has zero trace; a positive semidefinite one must be zero. This supplies a second finite algebra certificate. No compact-isometry Lie group theorem is needed in this proof.

## 3. The metric is a warped product

In the equivariant coordinates Lorentz now acts only on x. At each fixed y, write the external block H, mixed block C, and internal block g_K. Infinitesimal invariance for every Lorentz generator L requires L^T H+HL=0 and L^T C=0. The first system has a one-dimensional kernel spanned by eta; the second has zero kernel column by column. These ranks (9/10 and 4/4) are independently checked exactly. Thus H=c(y) eta and C=0. Nondegeneracy, positive definite internal metric, and index one imply c>0, so c=e^{2A} with A smooth. This proves g=e^{2A} eta +g_K globally in the lifted translation coordinates, although arbitrary starting coordinates may exhibit nonzero cross terms.

Explicit sharpness of the coordinate distinction: on R^4 x S^1, take the flat product eta+R² dtheta² and set theta=y+q_mu x^mu modulo 2pi with constant q. The resulting metric has nonzero mixed coefficient R² q_mu; determinant -R², and the Lorentz lift acts with compensating fiber shifts. The global change back to theta removes the mixed coefficient, and the field strength of this connection is zero. Exact matrix checks verify the conjugated Lorentz generators and the coordinate pullback. The metric is flat by diffeomorphism, not an independent gauge-field solution.

## 4. Vacuum consequence

Standard warped Ricci equations as derived in #260 give external Ric=-e^{2A} eta(Delta A+4|dA|²), hence Delta(e^{4A})=0. On connected closed K the maximum principle or integration by parts forces constant A. Then Ric_K=0. The integrated Killing Bochner identity forces every internal Killing field to be parallel; torsion-free connection makes their Lie brackets zero. The identity component of the compact internal isometry group is a torus. No non-Abelian SU3/SU2 Lie algebra can arise as the ordinary continuous Killing-vector KK gauge sector in this class. The non-block-diagonal loophole is closed under the stated full symmetry lift; this says nothing about off-diagonal dynamical fluctuations, non-Killing modes, gauge fields with nonzero background field strength breaking the assumed symmetry, approximate emergent Lorentz symmetry, noncompact fibers, or symmetry actions that close only up to internal gauge without an actual lifted Poincare action.

The metric is the only dynamical field assumed. No independent gauge fields, matter, spinors or flux introduced. No claim about recovering fermions, anomalies, quantum theory or stability. Diff. Geom. 10 (1975), 277–288. The specific lift argument here is an elementary synthesis; no priority claim.
