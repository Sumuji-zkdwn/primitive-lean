## Question and exact result

For β>0, χ periodic with period L>0, the previous nonflat solution on t>0 is
ds²=−dt²+(t²+β)γ_H3+t²/(t²+β)dχ².
Does finite curvature as t→0 imply a causally regular past extension?

Set r=sqrt(t²+β), s=sqrt(β), f(r)=1−β/r². On r>s, dr/dt=t/r=sqrt(f), hence ds²=−dr²/f+f dχ²+r²γ_H3.
Define r*=r+(s/2)log((r−s)/(r+s)), so dr*/dr=1/f, and v=χ+r*. The periodicity is v~v+L at fixed r. Substitution cancels the dr² term exactly:

    ds²=−2 dr dv+f(r)dv²+r²γ_H3.

This is a smooth Lorentzian metric for every r>0 on (0,∞)×S¹×H³. Its radial/circle block has determinant −1, one negative and one positive eigenvalue, even at r=s. In the H³ upper half-space chart γ_H3=(dx²+dy²+dz²)/z², z>0, the 5D determinant is −(r²/z²)^3. Direct symbolic differentiation verifies all 25 Ricci entries vanish, with full Riemann contraction K=72β²/r⁸. K at r=s equals 72/β². No independent matter or gauge fields were used.

However the compact orbit generated by ∂v has squared norm f(r), is closed because v is periodic, and:
- r>s: spacelike; original t>0 has a time function t.
- r=s: closed nonzero null curves, so causality already fails on the horizon.
- 0<r<s: closed timelike curves through each point, so chronology fails.

This explicit extension is smooth but not globally hyperbolic. A smooth continuation preserving this compact Killing orbit and crossing this simple zero of its norm has the same local sign obstruction. This is not a classification of all possible symmetry-breaking or nonanalytic extensions. Unwrapping v removes this specific closed-orbit argument but abandons the compact circle. No perturbative instability theorem is asserted.

For v,x,y,z constant, Γ^μ_rr=0 for every μ, so r itself is an affine parameter for a null geodesic. The original region ends at r=s at finite affine parameter. The explicit extension continues that geodesic, but ends at r=0 with K diverging at finite affine parameter. Thus neither the original region nor this extension is geodesically complete. Do not infer that all geodesics extend across the horizon. In particular the other radial null family winds infinitely in v near the horizon.

## Prior art and physical limitations

Jackson (2018), https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.09983 already gives the FRW×circle family; the causal mechanism is the standard Misner-space compact boost/chronology-horizon phenomenon, see Denaro and Dotti (2015), https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01092. Their perturbation results are not automatically applicable to this 5D curved metric. This is a reproducible application, not a novel general relativity solution. Local Lorentzian signature and classical vacuum covariance hold; global causal predictability fails on this extension. No physical stabilization, SM spectrum, chirality, quantum theory or experimental confirmation.
