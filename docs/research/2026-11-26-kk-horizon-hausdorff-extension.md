# 2026-11-26 — A smooth Hausdorff vacuum extension with both nonlinear curvature blow-up and closed timelike curves

## Claim and scope

Continue #279, on the original H^3 x S^1 data, with its specific smooth compactly supported non-CMC perturbations, independent of the circle coordinate. For every sufficiently small nonzero amplitude in that family, its maximal globally hyperbolic development (MGHD) admits a proper, connected, Hausdorff, second-countable, smooth, time-oriented Ricci-flat extension containing closed timelike curves. In that same extension the protected timelike geodesic of #279 still has unbounded invariant curvature at finite past proper time and no regular C^2 endpoint. In particular, local nonlinear curvature blow-up from arbitrarily small finite-norm perturbations does not imply global chronology protection or even C^2-inextendibility of the entire MGHD. This is an existence statement for a special symmetry-preserving family, not genericity or a claim about the observed universe.

## 1. Prior inputs and the distant regular patch

Fix beta=s^2>0, initial r0>s, periodic chi of period L>0. #279 provides exact compact vacuum data (g_epsilon,K_epsilon) on Sigma=H^3 x S^1 with g_epsilon=g0, K_epsilon-K0 compactly supported and arbitrarily small in every fixed finite Sobolev norm. The seed and the algebraic extrinsic-curvature correction are independent of chi. Its Cauchy development has an isometrically embedded singular homogeneous region, with a central comoving timelike geodesic gamma_s along which t^4 R_abcd R^abcd tends to a strictly positive constant as proper time t->0+. The nonlinear domain-of-dependence argument and invariant calculation are in #279/#277.

Because the perturbed data agree exactly with background outside a compact set, select a distant hyperbolic ball of radius R larger than R_*=acosh(r0/s), disjoint from the support, taking the entire circle. Choose a nonempty smaller open ball V in its interior with distance to the initial ball boundary strictly greater than R_*. On the original exterior the causal spatial displacement to Sigma from any r in (s,r0] is at most R_*, by #275. Thus local geometric uniqueness/domain of dependence as used in #279 gives an open isometric embedding of (s,r0) x S^1 x V, in the advanced coordinates v=chi+r*(r), into the MGHD M, identifying its metric with
    g=-2 dr dv+f(r) dv^2+r^2 gamma_H3,
    f(r)=1-s^2/r^2.
We use only the existence of this embedded open collar, not a global chart on M or a claim that the entire M is known analytically. Cauchy locality holds for the smooth Einstein vacuum equations in 4+1 dimensions.

## 2. Periodic Killing symmetry on the full MGHD

Translation chi -> chi+theta, theta in R/LZ, is a smooth circle group action preserving both initial tensors. By geometric uniqueness and maximality each such data automorphism extends to a unique time-orientation-preserving isometry Phi_theta of M. For instance apply MGHD uniqueness to the same development with reparametrized initial embedding; uniqueness of an isometry fixing the spacelike initial data and their future normal gives the group law and Phi_L=id. Smooth dependence of this action can be seen without assuming a global normal coordinate chart: on local Gaussian normal neighborhoods of Sigma the isometry is given by translating the initial point and applying the spacetime normal exponential. Propagate the smooth family of isometries and its differential along a finite chain of normal neighborhoods on a path to any given point of connected M using Phi_theta(exp_p u)=exp_(Phi_theta(p))(dPhi_theta u). Composition by Phi_theta gives joint smoothness near every group parameter. Thus there is a smooth global Killing generator Z with a periodic flow, with the parameter normalization fixed by chi.

The collar embedding is equivariant: the initial matched region includes the whole circle, and local uniqueness identifies its translations with the restrictions of Phi_theta (two isometries that agree on the initial region and preserve its future normal agree on its connected development). Hence Z=partial_v in the collar. Global hyperbolicity implies causality, so at any point of M with Z!=0 its squared norm must be strictly positive. If it were nonpositive, the periodic orbit would be a nonconstant closed causal curve, with a consistent time direction (the isometric flow preserves time orientation and the norm of Z). Z can have zeros in general compact actions; the next step handles that possibility rather than assuming a positive lower bound on its norm.

## 3. Exclude interior accumulation of the horizon: the key Hausdorff test

Let q_n=(r_n,v_n,X_n) in the embedded collar, r_n->s+. Suppose their images converge to a point p in M. The scalar g(Z,Z)=f(r_n) tends to zero, so g(Z,Z)(p)=0. By causality as above, Z(p)=0. Since the full flow fixes p, its derivative at p is a compact group of linear Lorentz isometries of T_p M preserving the future timelike cone. Average any future timelike vector under this compact group (Haar probability measure). The result is future timelike: the future cone is convex and the orbit compact, or use the reverse triangle inequality on the time components in an orthonormal frame. The averaged vector is fixed. Differentiating the tangent action shows that the endomorphism nabla Z at p annihilates this timelike vector; it is skew relative to g because Z is Killing. In an orthonormal frame with that time axis the covariant two-form F_ab=nabla_a Z_b has only spatial components. Therefore
    J(p)=F_ab F^ab(p) >= 0.

But in the embedded collar Z_flat=-dr+f dv, and the Killing identity gives F=(1/2)d Z_flat=(f'/2) dr wedge dv. The inverse r,v block is [[-f,-1],[-1,0]], so
    J=-f'(r)^2/2=-2 s^4/r^6 -> -2/s^2 < 0.
The invariant is continuous in the smooth MGHD, contradicting J(p)>=0. This argument excludes *any* interior accumulation point in M for sequences in the collar with r->s+, even if v or transverse coordinates vary. It does not confuse a vanishing orbit norm with a fixed point of the periodic action. The exact SymPy script checks the negative invariant, all components of F, and the real boost eigenvalues of its raised tensor.

## 4. Attach the collar and verify all manifold axioms

Choose 0<d<min(s,r0-s), and let U=(s-d,s+d) x (R/LZ) x V. Give all of U the above analytic collar metric. The determinant is -r^6/z^6 in upper-half-space H^3 coordinates, including at r=s. The base block has determinant -1, so the metric has Lorentz signature throughout. All 25 Ricci entries are identically zero for r>0, checked directly in this chart, not inferred from a distributional junction. A smooth timelike field on U is W=partial_r+partial_v, of norm f-2=-1-s^2/r^2, compatible with the original future orientation r increasing on U_+=U intersect {r>s}.

Write i:U_+ -> M for the open collar embedding in step 1. Form the quotient M_ext=(M disjoint_union U)/~ identifying q in U_+ with i(q), and no other points beyond the equivalence generated by these pairs. The quotient map is open: saturation of an open set adds only its corresponding image/preimage under the open overlap diffeomorphism. Its restrictions to M and U are injective open embeddings. To show Hausdorffness, use that the graph of i, considered as a subset of U x M, is closed. In these first-countable manifolds a possible limit pair (q,p) of graph points has q in U with r(q)>=s. For r(q)>s it is a graph point by continuity and Hausdorffness of M; for r(q)=s step 3 rules out convergence of i(q_n) to p. There is no limit pair with r(q)<s. Thus the graph is closed, as is the equivalence relation in (M disjoint_union U)^2 (diagonals plus the two cross-graphs). For inequivalent representatives the open complement of the relation gives product neighborhoods having no equivalent pair; their open quotient images separate the two classes. Hence M_ext is Hausdorff. Countable bases of M and U give a countable basis on the quotient via the open map. The two embeddings supply compatible smooth charts and the metric and time orientation agree exactly on the overlap. The quotient is connected since M and U are connected with nonempty overlap. It is a proper extension, since all points with r<=s in U are new. It has no boundary as a manifold (the collar interval and V are open). Local smoothness and vacuum Ricci-flatness hold at every point. No assertion that the extension is maximal among all extensions is made.

## 5. Coexistence in one extended spacetime

For any fixed r in (s-d,s) and X in V, the full periodic orbit v mod L has tangent Z=partial_v and g(Z,Z)=f(r)<0; the orbit is a smooth closed timelike curve entirely in the added region. Its orientation is future since g(W,Z)=f-1<0 there. Its proper length is L sqrt(-f). The original Cauchy slice is not a Cauchy surface of M_ext; this is not a contradiction to the MGHD theorem, which asserts maximality only among globally hyperbolic developments.

The isometric embedding of all of M preserves gamma_s and its curvature divergence, with Kstar>0 in #279. A finite-proper-time endpoint in any smooth or C^2 Lorentzian manifold would give a finite continuous Kretschmann scalar at that endpoint. Hence gamma_s cannot acquire such an endpoint in this extension; it remains past timelike geodesically incomplete. There is no claim that a singular point is itself a regular point of the manifold. In particular the same connected smooth metric has a curvature-singular incomplete direction and CTCs in another region, with arbitrarily small compactly supported exact initial perturbations and no extra field. The construction neither proves that every extension has a CTC nor rules out other extensions; it only constructs one.
