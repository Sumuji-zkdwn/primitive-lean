# 2026-11-26 — Periodic null vacuum horizons: first-jet rigidity and conditional one-way causality without assuming a Killing field

## Bounded result
Continue #281. Replace the circle-symmetric collar by the general smooth Gaussian-null form on a product chart (-d,d) x (R/LZ) x V, V an open connected three-dimensional screen patch. No circle Killing symmetry is assumed. The closed null generators, smooth null hypersurface, vacuum equations and (for nondegenerate normalization) positive mean nonaffinity are assumptions, not conclusions. Prove that the vacuum equations force the intrinsic screen metric to be independent of the periodic generator parameter, and after periodic reparametrization with positive constant nonaffinity force all first transverse metric jets to be independent of that parameter. Separately show that the one-way causal barrier of #281 survives in any sufficiently small such smooth collar with positive nonaffinity in the normalized chart, even with higher-order dependence on the periodic parameter. This is a necessary local rigidity result, not an existence or nonlinear stability theorem for symmetry-breaking vacuum perturbations.

## 1. General metric and horizon equations
Use coordinates u,v,x^A, A=1,2,3 with v modulo L and the future direction chosen so partial_u is future null. Let
    g = -2 du dv + u F dv^2 + 2u b_A dv dx^A + q_AB dx^A dx^B.
Equivalently:
    g_uu=0, g_uv=-1, g_uA=0, g_vv=u F, g_vA=u b_A, g_AB=q_AB.
F,b,q are arbitrary smooth periodic functions, q positive definite, and u=0 is the smooth null hypersurface H. Restrict V and d if necessary for these coordinate properties. In particular, the construction assumes each generator closes with the given screen label and common coordinate period, not that every possible extension has this form.

Write Q=q|_H, B=b|_H, S=partial_u q|_H, kappa=F|_H/2. The nonaffine geodesic generator Z=partial_v on H obeys nabla_Z Z=kappa Z. Use the optical matrix C^A_B=(1/2) Q^AC partial_v Q_CB, expansion theta=tr C, shear C-(theta/3)I. The twist vanishes because H is null. The exact Ricci equation on H is
    R_vv=-partial_v theta+kappa theta-tr(C^2).
This is the nonaffine null Raychaudhuri equation; tr(C^2)>=0 because C is self-adjoint for the positive screen metric. The oracle independently checks the equation in transverse Riemannian normal coordinates at an arbitrary horizon point: Q=I, Q_,A=0, Hmat=partial_v Q and Hmat_v=partial_v^2 Q are unrestricted there. The resulting expression is
    -tr(Hmat_v)/2 + tr(Hmat^2)/4 + F tr(Hmat)/4.
The normal-coordinate evaluation makes no assumption about transverse curvature or the u jets. It is consistent with the invariant formula above.

## 2. Non-expansion and zero shear, no constant kappa assumption needed
At fixed x, vacuum implies theta'<=kappa theta. With integrating factor exp(-integral kappa dv), a negative value theta(v0)<0 remains strictly negative throughout [v0,v0+L]. But theta=partial_v log sqrt(det Q), so periodicity gives integral over every full period theta dv=0. Contradiction. Thus theta>=0 everywhere, and its zero period integral and continuity imply theta=0 identically. Substitution in the exact vacuum equation gives tr(C^2)=0, hence C=0. Therefore
    partial_v Q_AB=0.
This uses smooth periodic positive-definite screen geometry and R_vv=0, not a pre-existing Killing field, compactness of the whole transverse space, or any sign of kappa. In particular an arbitrary nontrivial periodic shear on the horizon is incompatible with vacuum if the smooth closed-generator hypothesis is maintained. For a smooth family of metrics through our background, a first-order change of the intrinsic screen metric with nonzero v derivative cannot belong to such a family in generator-adapted coordinates. This does not rule out perturbations with a different boundary or generator structure.

## 3. Nonaffinity normalization and first transverse jet
Once Q is independent of v, direct Ricci calculation at H gives
    R_vA=(partial_v B_A-partial_A F|_H)/2,
    R_AB=Ric(Q)_AB+partial_v S_AB+kappa S_AB
           -(nabla^Q_A B_B+nabla^Q_B B_A)/2-B_A B_B/2.
The exact jet script verifies the three mixed and nine screen components in a transverse normal chart with arbitrary second screen derivatives, arbitrary S and its derivatives, and arbitrary F,B jets. Since the quantities have the indicated coordinate-transformation laws under screen-coordinate changes independent of v, the identities hold in the chosen Gaussian-null charts. R_vA=0 and periodicity imply partial_A integral_0^L kappa dv=0, so kappa_bar=(1/L) integral_0^L kappa dv is a constant on connected V.

For this nondegenerate branch assume kappa_bar>0, as at the #281 background (kappa=1/s). Solve for a positive periodic h(v,x) such that
    partial_v h + kappa h = kappa_bar.
With K(v)=integral_0^v kappa dw, use h(v)=exp(-K(v))(h(0)+kappa_bar integral_0^v exp(K(w))dw), where
    h(0)=kappa_bar integral_0^L exp(K(w))dw / (exp(K(L))-1).
It is positive, smooth, periodic (uniqueness of the ODE with periodic coefficients), and smooth in x. Z_new=h Z has nonaffinity kappa_bar because nabla_(hZ)(hZ)=(Z(h)+h kappa)hZ. Its period is still L: integrating h'/h+kappa=kappa_bar/h over a period gives integral_0^L dv/h=L. Choose the new generator parameter with this period and the same screen labels, and extend the coordinates off H using the transverse null normal normalized to g(partial_u,Z_new)=-1 and its affinely parametrized geodesics (Gaussian-null coordinates, after restricting to a sufficiently small collar on a relatively compact screen patch). The product structure of the horizon permits this periodic choice. No global extension of this coordinate system over the full development is asserted.

In the new chart Q remains v independent and F|_H=2 kappa_bar. Thus R_vA=0 yields partial_v B=0. The AB equation becomes
    partial_v S + kappa_bar S = -Ric(Q) + sym(nabla^Q B) + B tensor B / 2.
The right side is independent of v. For each screen point and component the general solution is the constant particular solution plus exp(-kappa_bar v) times a constant. Since exp(-kappa_bar L)!=1, periodicity forces the latter to vanish. Consequently partial_v S=0. We have shown Lie_(partial_v) g|_H=0 and partial_u Lie_(partial_v) g|_H=0 in this normalized chart: the intrinsic metric and first transverse metric jet must be generator-independent. No claim about all higher jets, a true Killing field in an open neighborhood, or a Killing symmetry of the full perturbed development. Zero mean nonaffinity is a separate case; the exponential periodicity argument then does not determine S.

## 4. One-way causal barrier without exact circle symmetry (conditional)
Assume a smooth Gaussian-null collar as above has F|_H=2 kappa_bar>0. Work on a relatively compact screen patch and a sufficiently small neighborhood of H. There q is positive and F-u|b|_q^2>0 by continuity. W=partial_u+partial_v is timelike since g(W,W)=-2+uF, and chooses the future direction. Every nonzero future causal Y has v'>=0, since g(partial_u,Y)=-v'. If v'=0 then Y is a positive multiple of partial_u. For v'>0 complete the screen square:
    g(Y,Y)=-2u'v'+u(F-u|b|_q^2)(v')^2
          +q(X'+u v' q^{-1}b, X'+u v' q^{-1}b).
Hence u'>=(1/2)(F-u|b|_q^2)v' u, also for v'=0. Along any compact locally Lipschitz causal segment in the chart the coefficient is integrable, and multiplication by its positive integrating factor forbids future crossing from u>0 to u<=0, or from u=0 to u<0. It does not rely on F,b,q being independent of v. Integral curves of W cross from u<0 to u>0. At fixed u<0,x the periodic v orbit is future timelike (g_vv=uF<0 and g(W,partial_v)=-1+uF<0). On u>0 the inverse-metric norm of du is -u(F-u|b|_q^2)<0; u increases along future causal curves there. These are local geometric results. If an extension of the full development exists with all its boundary in such collars, the first-exit argument of #281 then makes the old region a future set. The local inequalities alone do not establish Hausdorff attachment, persistence of a smooth null horizon under generic perturbations, or a global time orientation.

## 5. Interpretation, related work and next step
Smooth periodic horizon geometry is more restrictive than the existence of a general Lorentzian metric. The local optical and first-jet restrictions do not depend on the entire horizon being compact; here V may be noncompact or merely a patch. Closely related stronger global rigidity results require additional hypotheses: Petersen and Racz, Symmetries of vacuum spacetimes with a compact Cauchy horizon of constant non-zero surface gravity, https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02580 ; Petersen, Extension of Killing vector fields beyond compact Cauchy horizons, https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.09135 ; Reiris and Bustamante, On the existence of Killing fields in smooth spacetimes with a compact Cauchy horizon, https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08934 . Do not apply the compact-horizon theorems directly to this noncompact/transverse-local horizon. Raychaudhuri is standard, no general originality claimed. The contribution of this iteration is the explicit restricted Ricci-jet derivation and conditional barrier for our five-dimensional collar. The time orientation and null-generator topology remain assumed. No new exact nonsymmetric vacuum development, physical origin of time, thermodynamic arrow, generic chronology protection, quantum theory, or particle spectrum has been proved. The old symmetric examples #279–281 remain valid; one cannot declare generic collapse or generic stability from these local necessary conditions. Next finite task: examine higher transverse Taylor equations or explicit nonzero-frequency metric perturbations subject to the horizon conditions; distinguish formal rigidity, analytic rigidity, and smooth unique continuation. Retain the separate gravity and effective gel branches.
